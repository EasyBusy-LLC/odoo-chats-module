EasyBusy Chats

Professional omnichannel communication for Odoo. Manage WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber, Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WebChat conversations in one powerful interface.

EasyBusy Chats connects customer messages directly inside Odoo. Stop switching between tabs, reply faster, and keep all customer communication synchronized with your business workflow.

The module integrates with Odoo Discuss, supports CRM lead creation, and keeps a complete interaction history in the Chatter. It is built for sales, support, and operations teams that need clean communication without chaos.
